# Research And Verify

## Workflow

1. Restate the concrete question and identify facts that may have changed.
2. Prefer primary sources. Use independent reputable sources when a claim benefits from corroboration.
3. Record publication dates and distinguish when an article was published from when an event occurred.
4. Separate sourced facts, calculations, and inferences. Do not present an inference as a quoted fact.
5. Resolve meaningful conflicts by explaining which source is more authoritative or current.
6. Answer directly, cite the supporting pages near each claim, and note any material uncertainty.

## Guardrails

- Never invent a source, quotation, statistic, feature, or date.
- Do not expose credentials, private prompts, or unrelated user data in queries or output.
- Keep excerpts short; summarize copyrighted material instead of reproducing it.
- If live access is unavailable, state which claims remain unverified.
